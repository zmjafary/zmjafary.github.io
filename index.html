How I work

Four things I stick to.

These didn’t come from a book. They came from leading teams and then having to live with what I shipped afterwards.

01

I build for whoever has it open all day

An inspector standing in a yard, an admin closing a rota, an operator trying to list a tour. They’re the ones I picture, and the ones I go back and ask.

02

I don’t skip the boring parts

Roles and permissions, audit trails, queues, invoices, payment states. None of it’s fun and none of it demos well. But it’s what decides whether anyone still trusts the thing in year three.

03

I fix how things get shipped

Setting up CI/CD with Jenkins, Docker and Kubernetes at SwishTag took downtime down by 30%. I’d rather spend a week on the pipeline than keep losing hours to deploys that go sideways.

04

I make sure I’m not the only one who knows

I’ve mentored juniors at YumyApps, iCashCars and SwishTag. Onboarding got quicker and code quality went up 15%. Being the only person who understands a system just leaves a problem for whoever comes after you.
